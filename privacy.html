Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

zoneflood ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by zoneflood studio ("we") for the YouTube channels we own. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how we handle it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the app

Only zoneflood studio uses the app, and only with the YouTube channels it owns. It is not offered to the public, and it does not create accounts for, sign in, or collect data from anyone else.

Data the app accesses

  • Our own channels (with our authorization through Google OAuth). The app uploads videos, sets thumbnails and reads basic channel information (channel ID and title). It also reads YouTube Analytics reports for our channels, such as views, watch time and estimated revenue.
  • Public YouTube data. For market research, the app reads publicly available metadata and statistics of public videos and channels, such as titles, durations, view counts and thumbnail links, through the YouTube Data API.

The app does not access the content of private messages, comments of other users, viewers' personal information, or any Google account data beyond what is listed above.

How data is stored and used

  • Authorization tokens are stored only on the computer or server that runs the app, in files readable only by its operator. They are never shared.
  • Data from YouTube API Services is used only to publish to our channels, to decide what to publish, and to measure our channels' performance.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any of this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Data obtained from YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Analytics for our own channels is kept only as long as we need it to run those channels.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After access is revoked, we delete the related authorization tokens and stored data within 7 days. To request deletion of any data, email us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and our videos are not made for kids.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
