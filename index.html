zoneflood

A private publishing tool operated by zoneflood studio.

zoneflood studio runs a small group of YouTube channels that publish long-form, AI-generated instrumental music mixes for studying, focus, workouts, sleep and the seasons.

zoneflood is the internal tool we use to publish to those channels. It renders each mix and checks that it is original. It uploads videos only to channels zoneflood studio owns, after a person has reviewed and approved them. Every upload is disclosed as altered or synthetic content.

zoneflood uses YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public, and no other people's accounts are connected to it.
